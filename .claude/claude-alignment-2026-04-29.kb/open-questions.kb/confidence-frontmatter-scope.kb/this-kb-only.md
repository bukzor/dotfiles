# Candidate: this kb only

Apply the `source`/`status`/`see-also` frontmatter only within
`claude-alignment-2026-04-29.kb/` and any sub-kbs created during
follow-up sessions specifically for alignment work. Other kbs
(project incubators, design.kb, etc.) are not affected.

The fit is conservative: the convention proves itself in the
context where it was developed, accumulates evidence about whether
the differentiation actually helps future Claudes, and only then
expands.

The cost: the value of the convention is hardest to measure when
it has only one user. Two or three more kbs adopting it would
quickly reveal whether the categories (`firm`/`tentative`/
`exploratory` and `user`/`agent`/`mutual`) are useful or whether
they collapse in practice. Confining scope means deferring that
evidence.

This candidate is a local minimum: it works, but it doesn't pay
its full freight, because most of the value comes from the
convention being known/expected by future Claudes broadly.
