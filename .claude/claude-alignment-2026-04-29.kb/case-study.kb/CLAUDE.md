# Case Studies

Concrete session episodes that illustrate principles or failure modes
captured elsewhere in this kb. Each case study describes one
discrete sequence of turns: what was happening, what the prior
Claude did, what went wrong, and what the user did or said in
response.

## What belongs here

A case study belongs here when it is a coherent multi-turn episode
that exemplifies one or more patterns documented in
`../failure-modes.kb/` or `../user-conventions.kb/`, with enough
detail that a future Claude can recognize a structurally similar
situation in their own work. Use see-also frontmatter to link each
case study to the patterns it illustrates.

## What does NOT belong

- Single-turn errors with no surrounding context — those are better
  captured as failure modes directly
- Generic narrative summaries of the session
- Episodes whose lesson is already cleanly captured in a failure mode
  with sufficient session-evidence detail there
