# The operator's attention is priced at $150/hr

The operator's attention is priced at **$150 per hour**, set by the
operator, and that conversion is what makes every other number in this
ledger comparable to a human alternative.

> [!@bukzor] aa519804
>
> I'd bill myself at maybe $150/hr

$150/hr is **$2.50 per minute**, and that is the conversion that makes
every other number in this ledger comparable to a human alternative.

The figure is a self-assessment, not a market rate, and it is the right
kind of thing to be one: what is being priced is the opportunity cost of
this operator's attention, which only this operator can set.

Its precision does not matter much. The thresholds it feeds are ratios of
4x to 16x (`HOURLY_COMPARISON`), so a rate wrong by a third moves no
decision. A rate wrong by a factor of five would.
