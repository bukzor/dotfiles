# decision.kb — maintenance guide

When to spend agent attention instead of the operator's.

## What belongs here

A claim bearing on the choice: a cost term, a threshold, a rule about
which mode to reach for, or a reason the arithmetic does not govern.

This is where the operator's own rulings live — the rate, the modes, what
a cost model must include. Those are `standing: user` and quote the
operator directly; everything else here rests on them.

## What does NOT belong here

How accurately any input can be estimated -> `../estimation.kb/`. A claim
here may cite an accuracy figure but should not establish one.

Where a claim depends on a guessed parameter — review minutes, cleanup
minutes — sign it `agent` and name the guess in the body. Signing such a
claim `bare` is the specific dishonesty this collection is prone to.
