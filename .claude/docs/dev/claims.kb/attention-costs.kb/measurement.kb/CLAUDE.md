# measurement.kb — maintenance guide

Facts about the corpus, each reproducible by a command.

## What belongs here

A claim whose truth is settled by running something: a total, a rate, a
structural property of the transcript format, a cross-check between two
sources. Every file carries `verify:`.

## What does NOT belong here

An interpretation of a number — why it is that size, what to do about it.
That belongs in the theory whose vocabulary the interpretation needs.
The tell: if the claim would survive the number changing, it is not a
measurement.

Pricing tables and tool behaviour that the arithmetic depends on belong
here too, since they are checkable and everything rests on them.
