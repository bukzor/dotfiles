# cost-structure.kb — maintenance guide

How a token's price depends on when it enters the context.

## What belongs here

A claim about the mechanism: replay, caching, the entry-versus-lifetime
distinction, what caps context growth. These explain the measurements
rather than restating them, and they would change only if the billing
model changed.

## What does NOT belong here

- How to predict any of it in advance -> `../estimation.kb/`
- What to do about it -> `../decision.kb/`

Corpus totals stay in `../measurement.kb/`; cite them rather than
restating them here.
